
================================================================================
MICRO-BUDGET ECOMMERCE COST CALCULATOR - INDIA 2026
For: Solo Founder | No Inventory Space | No Manpower | Capital < ₹25,000
================================================================================

REALITY CHECK:
- You CANNOT compete on price with established sellers who manufacture or buy 5000+ units
- You MUST sell on convenience, bundling, speed, or micro-niche positioning
- Your first 3 products will likely FAIL. Budget for 5 test products minimum.

COST CATEGORIES EXPLAINED:

1. PRODUCT LANDED COST
   - Supplier base price (what factory/wholesaler charges)
   + GST paid to supplier (DEAD COST if supplier is unregistered and you can't claim ITC)
   + Inward shipping (courier from supplier to your home)
   - NOTE: If buying cash from Sadar Bazar/Chawri Bazar, you pay NO GST but get NO invoice.
     This is illegal for scale but common at micro-level. Risk: no warranty, no return to supplier.

2. PACKAGING
   - Poly mailer bag: ₹3-5
   - Bubble wrap: ₹2-4
   - Thank you card/insert: ₹1-2
   - Tape/label: ₹1-2
   - TOTAL: ₹8-12 per unit for low-ticket items

3. PLATFORM FEES (THE HIDDEN KILLER)
   Amazon:
   - Referral Fee: 8-20% depending on category (Electronics 13%, Kitchen 11%)
   - Closing Fee: ₹5-60 fixed per item depending on price band
   - EasyShip/FBA Shipping: ₹30-80 (Amazon charges you even if customer pays shipping)
   - Storage (FBA): ₹20-40/cubic foot/month (AVOID FBA with no money)

   Flipkart:
   - Commission: 5-15% category dependent
   - Fixed Fee + Collection Fee: ~₹15-30 per order
   - Shipping: ₹35-60 depending on zone and weight

   Own Website (Shopify/WooCommerce):
   - Payment Gateway: 2% + GST (Razorpay/Cashfree/PayU)
   - Shipping: ₹45-70 via Delhivery/Shadowfax/Xpressbees (you negotiate based on volume)
   - Platform: ₹0 (WooCommerce) or ₹2,000/month (Shopify Basic)

4. GST (GOODS & SERVICES TAX)
   - If selling on Amazon/Flipkart: MANDATORY registration regardless of turnover
   - If own website + turnover <₹20L/year: NOT required (but then you can't sell to GST-registered businesses)
   - Rate: 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% depending on product HSN code
   - If you buy from unregistered supplier: You pay GST under REVERSE CHARGE (dead cost)
   - If you buy from registered supplier: You pay GST but claim INPUT TAX CREDIT (ITC)
     - With ITC: GST is NOT a cost (you collect from customer, pay government, minus what you already paid)
     - Without ITC: GST is a REAL COST added to your product cost
   - THIS CALCULATOR assumes NO ITC (worst case, most realistic for beginners)

5. RETURNS & REPLACEMENTS (THE PROFIT DESTROYER)
   - Amazon/Flipkart return rate: 8-25% depending on category
   - Return processing: ₹15-25 per unit (inspection, repackaging, relabeling)
   - Replacement cost: You ship new unit + eat the cost of returned unit (if unsellable)
   - For low-ticket items (<₹300), Amazon often says "keep it, we refund" — YOU lose full cost
   - Reserve 5-10% of revenue for returns

6. ADVERTISING (OPTIONAL BUT PRACTICALLY MANDATORY)
   - Amazon PPC: ₹15-50 per click, conversion 5-15% → ₹100-300 per sale
   - Flipkart Ads: Similar
   - Facebook/Instagram: ₹5-20 per click, conversion 1-3% → ₹200-600 per sale
   - With ZERO ad budget, you rely on organic rank (takes 3-6 months) or social media hustle
   - THIS CALCULATOR assumes ZERO ad spend for inventory models, ₹25/unit for dropship

7. MISCELLANEOUS (THE INVISIBLE COSTS)
   - Thermal printer labels: ₹1-2 per label
   - Internet/electricity amortized: ₹2-3 per unit at low volume
   - Accounting/GST filing (if registered): ₹500-1,500/month → ₹10-30 per unit
   - Platform subscription (Shopify): ₹2,000/month → ₹40-80 per unit at low volume
   - Sample purchases for photography: ₹500-1,000 one-time
   - Photography (DIY phone): ₹0, (Professional): ₹3,000-5,000 per product

================================================================================
WORKING CAPITAL MATH (WHY YOU RUN OUT OF MONEY BEFORE MAKING PROFIT)
================================================================================

Example: You buy 50 phone stands at ₹53 all-in = ₹2,650 inventory cost
You list at ₹199 on Amazon.

Timeline of cash flow:
Day 0:  -₹2,650 (inventory paid)
Day 1:  -₹200 (packaging materials bought)
Day 3:  Customer orders 1 unit, pays ₹199 to Amazon
Day 5:  You ship the unit (Amazon EasyShip picks up)
Day 7:  Customer receives it
Day 14: Amazon settles ₹199 - ₹25.87 (referral) - ₹8 (closing) - ₹45 (shipping) = ₹120.13
         BUT Amazon holds 5-10% as reserve for returns = ₹11.40
         Net credited: ₹108.73

So for 14 days, your ₹53 product cost is stuck. For 50 units, that's ₹2,650 stuck for 2 weeks.
Meanwhile, you need to reorder before stock runs out.

REALITY: You need 2x-3x your inventory cost in working capital because:
- Money is stuck in inventory (unsold units)
- Money is stuck in platform settlement (sold but not yet paid)
- Money is stuck in returns (products coming back)
- Money is needed for reordering before first batch profits arrive

WHICH PLATFORM FOR YOUR SITUATION?
================================================================================

AMAZON:
+ Highest traffic, customer trusts Prime
+ EasyShip handles pickup/delivery (no warehouse needed)
+ You can store 50 units under your bed, Amazon picks up daily
- 13-25% commission + shipping + closing fees
- Strict returns policy (customer always wins)
- Must be GST registered
- High competition, price wars

FLIPKART:
+ Lower commission than Amazon in some categories
+ Smart Fulfilment allows home-pickup
+ Tier-2/3 customer base growing fast
- Lower ASP (average selling price) than Amazon
- Settlement slower (15-20 days)
- Returns can be brutal

OWN WEBSITE (Shopify/WooCommerce + WhatsApp):
+ ZERO commission (only 2% payment gateway)
+ You own customer data (can retarget via WhatsApp)
+ Can sell without GST if turnover <₹20L (but then no B2B sales)
+ Cash on Delivery (COD) possible via Delhivery/Shadowfax
- YOU handle all shipping, returns, customer service
- ZERO traffic — you must drive every visitor via Instagram/Reels/SEO
- COD has 20-30% return rate (customer refuses delivery)

MEESHO (Zero-inventory model):
+ ZERO inventory needed (you share catalog, supplier ships)
+ ZERO commission (you add your margin on top of supplier price)
+ Perfect for no-space, no-money situation
- Margins are 5-15% only (extremely thin)
- Quality control is zero (supplier ships direct, you never see product)
- Returns are your headache
- Brand building is impossible

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATION FOR YOUR SITUATION (Little Money, No Space, No Manpower)
================================================================================

PHASE 1 (Month 1-2): Validation with ₹5,000-8,000
- Pick 1 product category (kitchen gadget or phone accessory)
- Buy 20-30 units from local market (Sadar Bazar Delhi, or local wholesale)
- Store under your bed / in a corner
- List on Amazon + Flipkart (GST registration required — cost ₹3,000-5,000 one-time)
- Price at 2.5x-3x your landed cost minimum
- Ship via EasyShip/Smart Fulfilment (they pick up from your home)
- Do NOT run ads. Optimize listing with good photos (phone camera + natural light)
- Target: Sell 5 units/week. If not achieved in 30 days, liquidate at cost and pivot.

PHASE 2 (Month 3-6): Double Down or Pivot
- If product sells 5+/week consistently: Order 100 units, negotiate 10-15% price drop
- If not: Kill it. You lost ₹5,000, not ₹50,000. Pick next product.
- Repeat until you find 1 winner.

PHASE 3 (Month 6+): Scale
- Once you have 1 winner selling 20+/week: Consider own website for margin boost
- Use Amazon/Flipkart for traffic, website for repeat customers (WhatsApp retargeting)
- Hire part-time helper only when you hit 50 orders/week
