## Core Operational Workflow

### Step 1: Macro Tectonic Scanning & Signal Detection (Phase 1 & 2)
* **6 Macro Forces**: Economic (inflation/savings), Demographic, Cultural, Regulatory (BIS/FSSAI/CE), Tech, Climate.
* **11 Demand Signal Feeds**: Google Trends (Velocity > Volume), Autocomplete, TikTok Creative Center, Reddit/Quora, Amazon Movers & Shakers, 1688 Ranks, Exploding Topics, YouTube pain videos, Alibaba RFQs, Patents.
* **3-Source Confirmation Rule**: Must appear in 3+ distinct signal sources before moving forward.

### Step 2: 3-Star Review Defect Mining & Gap Scoring (Phase 2B & 4)
* Extract 500+ recent 2-star and 3-star reviews from top 3 competitors.
* Identify recurring mechanical, material, size, usability, or packaging flaws ($> 20\%$ frequency).
* Run the **6-Dimension Gap Matrix**:
  1. Price Gap (15%)
  2. Quality Gap (20%)
  3. Trust Gap (15%)
  4. Convenience Gap (15%)
  5. Niche Gap (20%)
  6. Creative Gap (15%)
* **Gate Check (G5)**: Weighted Gap Score must be $> 18 / 25$ (or Wildcard $15+$) to proceed.

### Step 3: Location-Specific Sourcing Cluster Mapping (Phase 3 & 6)
Map the product to its primary global industrial cluster to minimize landed cost and defect rate:
* **China - Yiwu (Zhejiang)**: Small commodities, fashion jewelry, household organizers, novelty gadgets.
* **China - Shenzhen / Dongguan (Guangdong)**: Consumer electronics, smart wearables, PCB hardware, LED lighting.
* **China - Guangzhou / Foshan**: Cosmetics, leather handbags, fast fashion, furniture, ceramics.
* **China - Ningbo / Cixi / Yongkang**: Kitchen appliances, fitness equipment, stainless steel tumblers.
* **India - Tirupur (Tamil Nadu)**: Knitted organic cotton apparel, activewear, basic garments.
* **India - Surat / Ahmedabad (Gujarat)**: Synthetic textiles, embroidery, denim, lab-grown diamonds.
* **India - Moradabad (UP)**: Brassware, copper drinkware, metal home decor, lighting.
* **India - Agra / Kanpur (UP)**: Leather footwear, belts, bags, saddlery.
* **India - Jaipur / Jodhpur (Rajasthan)**: Handcrafted jewelry, wooden furniture (Sheesham/Mango), stone crafts.
* **Vietnam (Ho Chi Minh / Hanoi)**: Technical footwear, performance apparel, solid wood furniture.
* **Nearshoring**: Mexico (for USA duty-free / 3-day transit), Turkey (for EU apparel/ceramics).

### Step 4: Unit Economics, 4 Stress Tests & Cash Cycle (Phase 6)
Always enforce the **Golden Margin Rule**:
$$\text{Retail MSRP} \ge 4\times \text{Landed COGS} \quad (\text{Gross Margin} \ge 75\%)$$

* **Stress Test 4 Scenarios**:
  1. Ad CPM rises by $+40\%$
  2. Return/RTO rate hits $15\%$
  3. Platform fees increase by $+5\%$
  4. Shipping/freight jumps by $+20\%$
* **Cash Cycle Formula**:
  $$\text{Cash Cycle Days} = \text{Inventory Days} + \text{Payout Delay Days} - \text{Supplier Credit Days}$$
* **Gate Check (G7)**: All stress margins must remain $> 10\%$ AND Cash Cycle must be $< 60$ days.

### Step 5: Pre-Product Validation & M1-M5 Tests (Phase 7 & 8)
* **Cessna Mode Pre-Product Smoke Test**:
  * Build landing page mockup + 5 direct-response UGC hooks.
  * Run a $150 (Rs 5,000–8,000) 5-day ad test.
  * **Kill Triggers**: Click-Through Rate (CTR) $< 1.5\%$ or Initiate Checkout (IC) $< 3\% \rightarrow$ **KILL IMMEDIATELY BEFORE ORDERING INVENTORY**.
* **Validation Tests M1 - M5**:
  * **M1**: Problem Interview (8/10 confirm acute pain).
  * **M2**: Concept Smoke Test (CTR $> 2\%$, IC $> 5\%$).
  * **M3**: Price Test (Van Westendorp price elasticity curve).
  * **M4**: Channel Test ($> 60\%$ sell-through of 50–100 pilot units in 14 days).
  * **M5**: Delivery Test (NPS $> 50$, customer complaint rate $< 5\%$).
